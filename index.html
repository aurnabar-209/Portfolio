Research

Undergraduate thesis.

Deep Learning · Biomedical Signals

Multi-Scale Attention BiLSTM for Patient-Independent Seizure Detection

A deep learning pipeline that detects epileptic seizures directly from EEG signals, designed to generalize across patients rather than memorize one. The model combines multi-scale feature extraction with an attention mechanism over a bidirectional LSTM, trained and evaluated on the CHB-MIT Scalp EEG Database under Leave-One-Subject-Out Cross-Validation (LOSOCV) — a stricter test that mimics real-world deployment on unseen patients.

With Swarup Mazumder, Abdullah Al Rohan, and Ameer Sohail Aurnab · Supervised by Nazneen Akhter
